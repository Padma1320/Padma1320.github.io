Projects

In Development · Autonomous Navigation

GeoNav-AI

Developing a geospatial-intelligence and autonomous UAV navigation stack in ROS 2 and PX4. A U-Net with a ResNet-34 encoder, developed using OpenEarthMap aerial imagery, performs nine-class pixel-wise terrain segmentation. Semantic predictions are used to identify and score safer landing regions from terrain class, semantic confidence and spatial clearance.

The selected landing region is converted into a ROS 2 navigation goal and integrated with LiDAR perception, occupancy mapping, EKF-based state estimation, A* path planning and PX4 closed-loop mission execution. Its perception → state estimation → environment representation → planning → control architecture reflects core building blocks shared with autonomous-vehicle and ADAS stacks, implemented here on a UAV simulation platform.

PyTorch · U-Net · ResNet-34 · ROS 2 · PX4 · Gazebo · LiDAR · EKF · A* · C++ · Python

Dual-Stage PCB Defect Detection

EV Battery Safety Monitoring

Developed an ESP32-S3-based battery monitoring and fault-response architecture using voltage, current and temperature sensing. A local finite-state machine keeps critical safety decisions at the edge, with sub-100 ms local fault response.

Research

Under Review · IEEE ICRA 2027

Progress-Aware Predictive Safety Filtering for Hierarchical Recurrent Reinforcement Learning in Multi-Vessel ASV Navigation

Extends my ASV work toward safe reinforcement-learning-based autonomous navigation in multi-vessel environments. The framework combines recurrent steering and speed PPO policies, a joint residual policy, receding-horizon predictive safety filtering, and progress-aware selection among already-admissible actions.

Across the seven-family validation suite, the proposed system achieved 87/90 successful trials (96.67%). In the 14-scenario controlled comparison, the full method achieved 14/14, compared with 9/14 for nominal recurrent PPO and 10/14 for classical LOS + CPA/TCPA. Results are simulation-validated; hardware-in-the-loop and sea-trial validation are not claimed.

Deep Reinforcement Learning · Recurrent PPO · LSTM · Predictive Safety · COLREGS-Aware Navigation
