“How do we give a machine the ability to turn sensor information into a decision, act on the physical world, observe what happened — and make the next decision better?”

That question connects my background in sensors, signals and closed-loop control with my work in robotics, perception and reinforcement learning. I am especially interested in autonomy where intelligence must contend with real dynamics, uncertainty, safety constraints and hardware — not only software agents.

02 / RESEARCH

Making learned autonomy safer.

SUBMITTED · ICRA 2027 / RAS

Progress-Aware Predictive Safety Filtering for Hierarchical Recurrent Reinforcement Learning in Multi-Vessel ASV Navigation

Separate recurrent steering and speed policies propose nominal actions; a recurrent joint residual policy coordinates bounded corrections; a predictive safety filter screens steering-speed candidates before execution. Progress-aware ranking is applied only after safety admissibility.
