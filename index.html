04 / RESEARCH

Safety before execution.

MANUSCRIPT · SUBMISSION STAGE

Progress-Aware Predictive Safety Filtering for Hierarchical Recurrent Reinforcement Learning in Multi-Vessel ASV Navigation

Separate recurrent steering and speed PPO policies propose nominal actions. A recurrent joint residual policy coordinates bounded corrections, while a receding-horizon safety filter screens steering-speed candidates before execution. Progress ranks only actions that are already safety-admissible.
